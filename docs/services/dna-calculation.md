# Developer DNA Computation & Weighting Algorithms

## Repository Weighting Matrix
Repositories are weighted according to their depth and freshness:
- Recent repositories (< 6 months old): 1.5x multiplier
- Medium repositories (6 - 24 months old): 1.0x multiplier
- Legacy repositories (> 24 months old): 0.5x multiplier
- Pinned / Starred repositories: 1.25x bonus

This prevents outdated hobby projects from skewing current engineering capabilities.
